Every API task is paid for with **API credits**. You get credits from a monthly API plan and, on a paid plan, from top-up credit packs. New accounts don't include free credits, so subscribe to a plan before your first call.

<Note>
  A membership or credits on the PixVerse web app (app.pixverse.ai) don't apply
  to the API, and API credits can't be used in the web app. Buy API plans and
  credits on the [PixVerse
  Platform](https://platform.pixverse.ai/login?redirectPath=%2Fbilling).
</Note>

## Plans

Your account is on one plan at a time. The plan decides your monthly credits, concurrency limit, highest output quality, included features, and whether you can buy top-up credits. The **Billing** page is the current source for plans and prices.

| Plan      | Price  | Monthly credits |
| --------- | ------ | --------------- |
| Essential | $100   | 22,250          |
| Scale     | $1,500 | 360,000         |
| Business  | $6,000 | 1,636,365       |

Plans are subscriptions paid through Stripe. They renew automatically every 30 days until you cancel. Monthly credits reset every month. Paid plans can also buy top-up credits. Free accounts can't buy top-up credits.

For custom pricing, volume discounts, more or custom effects, or a higher concurrency limit, click **Let's talk** on the **Custom Plan** card, or email [api_business@pixverse.ai](mailto:api_business@pixverse.ai).

## Subscribe to a plan

<Steps>

  <Step title="Open Billing">

    Open [Billing on the platform](https://platform.pixverse.ai/login?redirectPath=%2Fbilling) and sign in.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a plan">

    Under **Find the right API plan**, compare the plans and click **Purchase** on the one you want.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay">

    Complete payment on the Stripe checkout page. Billing then shows your plan and credits under **Subscription Details**.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## How credits are charged

- **C1 and V6** charge per second of video: the rate times the `duration` you request.
- **Legacy models** (V5.6 and earlier) charge a fixed price per clip, by quality and duration.
- **Editing, audio, avatar and agent features** have their own rates.
- Tasks that fail moderation (status `7`) or fail to generate (status `8`) are refunded.

## V6

Text to video, image to video, first and last frame, extend, and reference to video with images. Credits per second:

| Quality | Without audio | With audio |
| ------- | ------------- | ---------- |
| 360p    | 5             | 7          |
| 540p    | 7             | 9          |
| 720p    | 9             | 12         |
| 1080p   | 18            | 23         |

The [Quick Start](/quickstart) and [first and last frame guide](/guides/first-last-frame) use V6, 5 seconds, 540p without audio. Each request costs **35 credits**. A 5-second V6 video at 720p without audio costs **45 credits**.

## Check your balance

The **Subscription Details** card on Billing shows **Remaining API Credits**, **Monthly Credits** and **Top-up Credits**. When your balance is too low for a task, the generation request fails with `ErrCode` `500090`. Add credits and submit again with a new `Ai-trace-id`.
