## How video generation works

<Steps>

  <Step title="Submit a request">

    Send a prompt, image, or other input to the endpoint for your workflow. The response gives you a task ID while generation continues in the background.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the task">

    Poll [Get video task status](/api/video-task-status) with the ID, or set up a webhook to be notified when the task finishes.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the result">

    When the task succeeds, read the result URL and download the finished video. [Quick Start](/quickstart) includes a script that runs all three steps.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Audio, avatars, agents and images

Add sound effects to video, match a character's mouth movement to speech, manage voices, or turn a portrait into a talking video. Agent workflows recreate viral videos with your assets or make a real estate video from a listing. Image templates generate images from a template and your photos.
